Why validate skills, not just job titles

Employers consistently report that validating skills and competencies is their single biggest hiring and workforce challenge — ahead of even sourcing candidates (McKinsey & Rework America Alliance, 2022). A skills-based approach to redesign makes that validation concrete: instead of redrafting a job description from scratch, you start from the government's own competency data, decide what the redesign actually changes, and check your people against it before you commit to a hiring or training plan.
