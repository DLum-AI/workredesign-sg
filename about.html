Where this data comes from, and its limits

Source data

SkillsMap.sg is built entirely on three public datasets published by SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) and Workforce Singapore (WSG) as part of the national Skills Framework:

  • Skills Framework dataset — job role descriptions, critical work functions, key tasks, and the Technical Skills & Competencies (TSC) / Critical Core Skills (CCS) each role requires, with proficiency levels, across 39 sectors and roughly 2,000 job roles.
  • TSC-to-unique-skills mapping — how each of the 12,000+ sector-specific TSC/CCS codes maps onto a smaller, de-duplicated taxonomy of skills.
  • Unique Skills List — the 2,388-entry taxonomy itself, including which skills are flagged as "emerging" or "CASL" (critical or advanced skills for the future economy).

All three were joined by their shared TSC/CCS codes to build this site's data files. Nothing here is modified from the source content — job role descriptions, task lists, and skill titles/descriptions are quoted as published.

Why a skills-based approach

McKinsey's research with the Rework America Alliance found that employers rank validating skills and competencies as their top hiring and talent challenge (48% of respondents), just ahead of sourcing candidates with the right skills (46%) — and that skills-based hiring is roughly five times more predictive of job performance than hiring on education alone. Their guidance on rolling out a skills-based approach (sourcing → hiring → career development) shaped how this tool sequences a redesign: define what the role actually needs before deciding who can do it.

Source: "Taking a skills-based approach to building the future workforce," McKinsey & Company, November 2022.

What this tool does not do

  • It doesn't automatically decide which skills a task removal affects — the source data links skills to job roles, not to individual tasks, so step 2's task checklist is a planning record for your report, not an automated skill-remover.
  • It doesn't verify anyone's actual proficiency — the validation step captures your own self-assessment of your team, which is a starting point for a conversation or a formal skills audit, not a substitute for one.
  • It isn't an official SkillsFuture or Workforce Singapore product, and isn't affiliated with either agency. Always check the official Skills Framework portal for the current, authoritative version of any role's skills before using it for compliance, grant applications, or formal HR decisions.

Privacy

This is a static site with no backend and no accounts. Everything you enter — the role you picked, tasks you flagged, and your team's self-assessed proficiency — stays in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, logged, or shared. Clearing your browser data or using a different device starts fresh.
