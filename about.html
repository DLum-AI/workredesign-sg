Why this tool exists

Most hirers don't skip job redesign on purpose — they've just never had a reason to look at a role task-by-task. This tool exists to create that moment, then connect what comes out of it to funding and to people.

The problem this solves

Automation and AI adoption are changing what jobs look like across Singapore SMEs — but buying a tool without redesigning the job around it is one of the most common reasons digitalisation spend fails to show up as productivity. The fix isn't more software. It's looking at the job itself first: which tasks should be dropped, which can be automated, and which genuinely need a person's judgement or relationships.

Once that's clear, many roles turn out not to need a full-time hire at all — they fit a fractional, job-share, or project-based arrangement instead, often filled well by an experienced mid-career PMET.

The five stages

StageWhat happens
1. AwarenessA 2-minute check on whether a specific role shows real redesign signals.
2. EducationA guided worksheet to break the role into its actual tasks.
3. ExecutionEach task is classified — eliminate, automate, augment, or elevate — and the leftover work is matched to an arrangement type.
4. Grant mappingThe redesign (and the resulting hire) is matched to the Singapore government schemes that can fund it, with a readiness checklist for each.
5. MatchA request goes to our team, who match it against vetted mid-career PMETs looking for exactly this kind of role.
